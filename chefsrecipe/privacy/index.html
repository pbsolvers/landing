본 앱은 계정 없이도 모든 기능을 이용할 수 있습니다. 로그인은 선택 사항이며, 인앱 결제는 없습니다.

The App is fully playable without an account. Signing in is optional, and there are no in-app purchases.

1. 개요

pbsolvers(이하 "개발자")는 모바일 게임 셰프의 레시피(Chef's Recipe, 이하 "본 앱")를 제공하며, 이용자의 개인정보를 소중히 다룹니다. 본 방침은 본 앱이 어떤 정보를 수집하고 어떻게 이용하는지 설명합니다.

2. 수집하는 정보

2-1. 로그인하지 않고 이용하는 경우 (게스트)

개발자에게 직접 전송되는 계정 정보가 없습니다. 게임 진행 상황(클리어한 스테이지, 점수, 별, 소리·언어 설정)은 이용자의 기기 안에만 저장되며, 앱을 삭제하면 함께 사라집니다.

다만 로그인 여부와 관계없이 제2-3항의 분석 정보와 제2-4항의 광고 정보는 수집됩니다.

2-2. Google 또는 Apple 계정으로 로그인한 경우

로그인 시 해당 서비스로부터 아래 정보를 받아 저장합니다.

2-3. 앱 이용 분석

본 앱은 Google Analytics for Firebase를 이용해 앱 이용 현황을 분석합니다. 이는 이용자를 개인으로 식별하기 위한 것이 아니라, 어느 단계에서 이용자가 어려움을 겪는지 파악해 게임을 개선하기 위한 것입니다.

항목 이용 목적

광고 시청 제안 및 수락 여부광고 배치 개선

2-4. 광고

본 앱은 Google AdMob을 통해 광고를 노출합니다. 광고 제공 과정에서 Google이 기기 정보와 기기의 광고 식별자를 처리할 수 있습니다.

본 앱은 앱 추적 투명성(ATT) 권한을 요청하지 않으며, 맞춤형 광고를 위한 추적을 하지 않습니다. 모든 광고 요청은 비개인화 방식으로 전송됩니다.

비개인화 광고에서도 광고 노출 빈도 제한, 부정 클릭 방지, 집계 리포팅 목적으로 기기의 광고 식별자가 이용될 수 있습니다. 또한 광고 제공 과정에서 IP 주소로부터 추정된 대략적인 위치(국가·도시 수준)가 이용될 수 있습니다.

1. Overview

pbsolvers ("we") develops the mobile game Chef's Recipe ("the App"). This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Playing without signing in (guest)

No account information is sent to us. Your progress (cleared stages, scores, stars, sound and language settings) is stored only on your device and is removed when you delete the App.

However, the analytics data in 2.3 and the advertising data in 2.4 are collected whether or not you sign in.

2.2 Signing in with Google or Apple

Data Purpose
Email addressAccount identification, support
Name / nicknameDisplay in the App
Profile image URLDisplay in the App
Sign-in provider (Google / Apple) Account identification
Preferred language, time zoneDisplay settings
Last activity timestampCleaning up inactive accounts
Game records (stage, score, stars, played at) Progress backup and cross-device sync

If you use Sign in with Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive an Apple relay address (@privaterelay.appleid.com) and never see your real address.

2.3 Usage analytics

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how it is played. The purpose is not to identify you, but to find where players get stuck so the game can be improved.

Data Purpose
App instance ID (an anonymous identifier generated by Firebase) Avoiding double counting
App opens, closes and session length Measuring frequency and return rate
Stage events (start, clear, fail, score, duration, failure reason) Difficulty tuning
Whether a rewarded ad was offered and accepted Improving ad placement
Device model, OS version, language, country/region Understanding the install base
App version, signed-in state (guest or signed in) Comparing versions and cohorts

The app instance ID is not combined with your name, email or any other identifying information, and it is reset when you delete the App. Country and region are estimated from your IP address; precise location is not collected.

2.4 Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may process device information and the device's advertising identifier to deliver them.

The App does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not track you for personalized advertising. All ad requests are sent as non-personalized.

Even for non-personalized ads, the advertising identifier may be used for frequency capping, fraud prevention and aggregate reporting. Ad delivery may also use an approximate location (country or city level) derived from your IP address.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from partner sites and apps

2.5 What we do not collect

Camera, microphone, photo library (the App does not request these permissions), precise location (the App does not request location permission), contacts, phone number, payment information (the App has no in-app purchases), and push notification tokens (the App does not use push notifications).

3. How We Use Information

Account authentication; progress backup and cross-device sync; responding to support requests; usage analysis and game improvement as described in 2.3; and serving ads as described in 2.4.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

4. Service Providers

We do not share personal information with anyone else, except where required by law through lawful process.

4.1 International transfers

Both providers are located outside the Republic of Korea, so the data entrusted to them is transferred abroad — to the United States and other countries where Google and Apple operate data centres — over the network as you use the App.

5. Retention and Deletion

Account information and game records are retained while your account exists. When you request account deletion, we delete your account information and the game records linked to it. Guest progress stored only on your device is removed as soon as you delete the App. Analytics data is deleted automatically once the retention period configured in Google Analytics elapses.

6. Your Rights

  • Sign out: in the App, Settings → Account → Sign Out
  • Access, correction, deletion: contact us at the address below
  • Account deletion: email us from or with the address you signed up with, and we will process it within 7 business days
  • Stopping analytics collection: deleting the App stops collection and resets the app instance ID. To have already-collected analytics data deleted, contact us at the address below.

Deleting your account also deletes your backed-up progress and cannot be undone. You can continue playing as a guest.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 (under 13 where applicable), and we delete such information as soon as we become aware of it.

8. Security

All communication with our servers is encrypted with HTTPS. We never store your password — authentication is handled entirely by Google and Apple.
